The mesh is stored separately from map objects and is generated only after clicking “Generate Mesh”. tower and impassable_terrain are impassable; grass remains passable but participates in boundary splitting; jungle_spot is currently ignored. Overlap priority is defined by the type registry.

No mesh generated.

Quality Parameters

The mesh generator first inserts vertices along overly long constrained straight edges, then performs constrained Delaunay triangulation and refines high-aspect-ratio or small-angle triangles using longest-edge bisection and circumcenter/Steiner points. The current maximum is 5000 cells; refinement stops when the budget is reached. A small number of thin triangles forced by acute source geometry may remain.

Visualization / Visualization

Topology wiki — meanings, formulas and usage ↗

Visualizations are derived from the mesh and do not modify the map or mesh. The built-in “Reachable Distance Field” computes shortest-path distance from a source over the passable-cell adjacency graph and colors all reachable areas using a red-to-blue continuous legend.
